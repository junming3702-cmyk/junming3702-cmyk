## Featured project

### [Evidence-grounded AI for construction tendering](https://github.com/junming3702-cmyk/-LLM)

A research prototype that connects procurement-document review to traceable evidence, deterministic checks and human judgment.

- Retrieves evidence with source hierarchy and applicability checks.
- Keeps unsupported claims and missing information visible for reviewers.
- Produces structured findings and supports export for human review.
- Includes public code, synthetic examples and reproducible functional checks.

## How I contribute

I independently conceived, designed and developed the construction-tendering pre-review project. I defined its objectives and full architecture, designed the legal-authority RAG hierarchy and ordered L1–L4 retrieval with BM25 + dense search, and developed the reasoning workflow and deterministic release gates. I also designed the evaluation plan, reviewed outputs and refined the program.

AI tools assisted code implementation and experimental table preparation under my direction. Domain practitioners appraised outputs and contributed interview feedback to evaluation design. [Explore my design contributions and the implementation](https://github.com/junming3702-cmyk/-LLM#my-contribution-and-collaboration).

## Research direction

I start with **industry pain points** and ask how AI can help make workflows more digital, decisions more traceable, and work more productive. Construction and engineering management are my primary application domains.

As methods mature, I want to test their transfer to other sectors and identify which adaptations are needed. Productivity gains and cross-domain transfer are research goals to be evaluated.
